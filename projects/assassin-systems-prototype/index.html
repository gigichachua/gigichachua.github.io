Assassin-style Systems Prototype

Assassin-style UE5 prototype: custom parkour + integrated combat + horse traversal that feels like one coherent game.

2:10 Play video: Assassin-style prototype, 2:10
Year
2023–2024
Built with
  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Blueprints
  • Motion Warping
  • Animation Systems

I built this prototype in Blueprints as freelance work at EvoGen. The goal was to take very different gameplay systems and make them work together as one stable experience.

The parkour system is my own: ledge climbing with shimmying, corner turns and hops, mantling with motion warping, vaulting, sliding, rolling, and walking along balance beams, plus the ledge and beam detection behind them. I also added movement rules, UI feedback, and safety checks so the character would not get stuck in broken states. The animations themselves come from Mixamo and animation packs.

I then integrated a third-party system, Flexible Combat System, by porting my parkour into its player character and adding horse riding from a horse pack. That meant getting animations, meshes, and mounting and dismounting to behave correctly across combat and horse movement. A teammate built the first map blockout, and the environments use Marketplace packs.
