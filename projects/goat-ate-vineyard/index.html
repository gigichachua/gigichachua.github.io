GoatAteVineyard

Global Game Jam project inspired by a Georgian poem, where the player masks into different characters to progress through a chain of absurd fights.

1:04 Play video: GoatAteVineyard gameplay, 1:04
Year
2026
Built with
  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Blueprints
  • Behavior Trees
  • Niagara

Built during Global Game Jam Georgia with a small team, this project was inspired by the Georgian poem "თხამ ვენახი შეჭამა".

The core idea was that the player keeps changing identity, masking into different characters to move forward. Each form is needed to defeat the next enemy, so progression becomes a chain of transformations based on the poem.

The tone quickly becomes absurd in the best way: cartoon combat, strange characters (like walking and punching gun), and escalating chaos, all leading to the joke that no matter how well you play, the goats are simply too strong, therefore "თხამ ვენახი ვერ შეჭამა", GoatCouldNotEatVineyard.

I made it with Shota Khakhishvili, who built the fighter and mask framework, the UI, and the level. My part was the enemy AI (a Behavior Tree with territories, patrols, and checks for the player entering them), switching off distant AI for performance, the cartoon explosion effects, the sound pass, and integrating six new characters after the jam.
