Singularity

UE5 game jam shooter, extended after the jam: enemies react to sight and gunshots, with a healing drone, upgrades, and puzzle-gated levels.

2:32 Play video: Singularity gameplay, 2:32
Year
2022–2025
Built with
  • Unreal Engine 5
  • C++
  • Blueprints
  • Behavior Trees

Singularity started as a game jam project in December 2022, made with Tornike Todria, and I kept extending it through 2025. You play an AI fighting up through five spaceship levels with a healing drone, timed upgrades, and puzzle-locked doors.

I handled the gameplay programming, and a level designer handled the map layout. The C++ base (shooter character, gun, game mode, and enemy Behavior Tree tasks) came from the shooter project in GameDev.tv's Unreal C++ course. On top of it I built the drone companion, the upgrade screen and save system, the rotating-code and pressure-plate puzzles, enemy patrols and view-cone checks, gunshot noise that sends enemies to investigate, ammo pickups, and upper-body aiming with turn-in-place. The 3D assets came from Marketplace packs.

The goal was to make the different systems work together as one clear player experience instead of feeling like separate mechanics placed side by side.
